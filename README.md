## My favourite math formulas

Here are some of my favourite math formulas:

$$
y = \alpha x^2 + \beta x + \gamma
$$

This is the parabolic function: $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$ are coefficients, and $x$ is the input variable.

$$
y = \frac{k_1}{k_2 x + c}
$$

This is a hyperbolic function: $k_1, k_2$ are coefficients, $x$ is the input variable, $c$ is a constant.

$$
D = b^2 - 4ac, \quad x_1 = \frac{-b + \sqrt{D}}{2a}, \quad x_2 = \frac{-b - \sqrt{D}}{2a}
$$

This is how to solve a quadratic equation: $D$ is the discriminant, $a, b, c$ are the equation's coefficients, $x_1, x_2$ are the solutions.

$$
\begin{cases}
2x + 3y - z = 7 \\
4x - y + 2z = 4 \\
-x + 2y + 5z = 11
\end{cases}
$$

This is a system of linear equations: $x, y, z$ are the unknown variables.

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{n} i = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}
$$

This is the formula for the sum of the first $n$ positive integers, where $i$ is the summation index.

$$
f(x) = \frac{1}{\sigma\sqrt{2\pi}} e^{-\frac{(x-\mu)^2}{2\sigma^2}}
$$

This is the normal distribution density: $\mu$ is the mean, $\sigma$ is the standard deviation, $x$ is the random variable.

$$
e^{i\varphi} = \cos\varphi + i\sin\varphi, \qquad A\vec{v} = \lambda\vec{v}, \qquad T = \frac{2\pi}{\omega}, \qquad \theta = \omega t
$$

Euler's formula ($\varphi$ is an angle), the eigenvalue equation ($\lambda$ is an eigenvalue of matrix $A$ with eigenvector $\vec{v}$), and the period $T$ of oscillation with angular frequency $\omega$, where $\theta$ is the angle at time $t$.

$$
I_n =
\begin{bmatrix}
1 & 0 & \cdots & 0 \\
0 & 1 & \cdots & 0 \\
\vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\
0 & 0 & \cdots & 1
\end{bmatrix}
$$

This is an identity matrix of size $n \times n$.
